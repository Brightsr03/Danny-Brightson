Our Story

Make Every Journey an Unforgettable Memory

The world is a book — and those who do not travel read only a page.

Since 1996, Sri Alagarmalaiyan Bus Transports has been the most trusted name in travel across Tamil Nadu. Founded on the values of safety, comfort, and reliability — we have delighted over one lakh passengers, from school students on their very first excursions to families on pilgrimages and corporate teams on retreats.

Our fleet of 20+ brand-new air-conditioned coaches, driven by experienced professionals with 10+ years on the road, ensures that every passenger arrives safely, comfortably, and on time — every time.
